Arthur Fouray Systems

Arthur Fouray Systems is the archive and index of Arthur Fouray's art, rt4a works, music, exhibition making, screen projects, mmm, a plus o minus, Pre-Natal, and before Arts.

Arthur Fouray (b. 1990, Paris) is a French artist, curator and musician. He studied at ECAL in Lausanne and has held solo exhibitions at the Aargauer Kunsthaus, Aarau; ZQM, Berlin; La Placette, Lausanne; and Espace Quark, Geneva. He co-founded the artist-run spaces Silicon Malley, Prilly, and co-directed DOC!, Paris. He is an archivist and curator at LUMA Arles. He lives and works in Paris.

Arthur Fouray Systems

Arthur Fouray Systems is the archive and index of Arthur Fouray's art, rt4a works, music, exhibition making, screen projects, mmm, a plus o minus, Pre-Natal, and before Arts.

Arthur Fouray (b. 1990, Paris) is an artist and curator whose practice explores the systems of narration, production, and distribution of art. In 2015, he co-founded the artist-run space Silicon Malley in Prilly (Switzerland) and subsequently joined the curatorial team at DOC! in Paris (2016-2018). Since 2019, he has held the position of Archivist & Curator at LUMA Arles, where he is in charge of the Hans Ulrich Obrist Archives.
